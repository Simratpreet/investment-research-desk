# Piccadily Agro — IMFL Portfolio Summary

| Brand | Category | Expressions | Price Range | FY26 Growth | Strategic Role |
|-------|----------|-------------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Indri** | Indian single malt whisky | Trini (core), Dru (cask strength), Founder's Reserve 11YO, Rudhira (252 bottles), HotD collab, City Series (travel retail), Triple Cask, Diwali editions | ₹4,000–12,500 | 16% vol | Flagship. Brand equity, premium margins (45–50% EBITDA), global top-15 single malt. Supply-constrained in FY26. |
| **Whistler** | Barrel aged blended malt whisky | Single SKU (recently revamped branding + formulation), 42.8% ABV | ₹750–1,500 | 98% vol | Volume engine. Mid-premium segment. Target: 1M cases in 3 years. Fastest-growing brand in portfolio. |
| **Camikara** | Pure cane juice rum (agricole style) | 3YO, 8YO, 12YO (flagship, 50% ABV, ~$90 US) | ₹1,500–5,000+ | 11% vol | Niche/collector. India's first agricole rum. International credibility (Gold at Rum & Cachaça Masters). Low volume, high brand halo. |
| **Cashmur** | Crafted vodka | Single SKU (launched May 2025) | Not disclosed | New launch | White spirits category entry. Early seeding phase. "Encouraging response" per management, no data yet. |
| **Royal Highland** | IMFL whisky (legacy) | Details not disclosed | Budget tier | Not disclosed | Legacy/regional. Rarely discussed. Minimal strategic focus. |

**FY26 IMFL revenue:** ~₹483 cr (42.6% of distillery segment). Blended IMFL volume growth: 48% YoY. Pipeline: new premium/luxury launches in "categories we're not currently in."

The key takeaway from the portfolio structure: Indri builds the brand ceiling, Whistler delivers the volume floor, and Camikara/Cashmur fill out category breadth. The 48% blended IMFL volume growth in FY26 was heavily driven by Whistler's 98% surge — Indri at 16% was the laggard, though that's set to reverse as supply constraints ease in FY27.
