**Expansion**
- The Indri distillery expansion was completed in October 2025, scaling ENA and ethanol capacity from 78 KLPD to 220 KLPD and malt capacity from 12 to 30 KLPD, as well as added barrels for maturation at the Indri distillery. 
- Our greenfield distillery in Mahasamund, Chhattisgarh, with a 200 KLPD capacity was commissioned in December 2025. Our total distillery capacity now stands at a robust 450 KLPD, comprising of 30 KLPD of malt and 420 KLPD of ENA and ethanol. 

**Notes**
- The management is saying that Indri plant will add 250-300 crore in revenue in FY27. 
- The Chhattisgarh plant will add 300-400 crore revenue on extra 200 KLPD capacity
- Overall revenue growth guidance is 60-70%. Management said that they were supply-constrained last year and with these expansions, they will be able to trigger more volume growth and bring new products into the pipeline. 
- In the next 3-5 years, they want 50% of the overall business to be export. Currently almost 45 to 50% is the domestic market. Then 25% is export and 30% is CSD. 
- Margins next year should be the same or slightly better than this year. IMFL margins are around 45-50% currently. 
- 3–4x current revenue in 3–4 year
- Brand ambition for Indri: Currently a top-15 global single malt. Management targets top-10 within a year or two, and top-5 globally within 3–5 years.
- Whistler volume target: 1 million cases within 3 years (from the Business Standard interview, April 2026). This is the mid-premium volume play at ₹750–1,500/bottle.
Barrel maturation runway: Scaling from 83,800 barrels currently to 100,000 by March 2027. All barrels are reserved for own IMFL brands — no bulk malt sales. This barrel inventory essentially pre-determines the IMFL revenue capacity 3–5 years out, since single malt needs ageing time.
