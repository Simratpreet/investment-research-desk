# FTG — Q1 FY26

**Headline Numbers**
- Revenue up 10%
- Bookings: C$60M (up 17% YoY)
- Quarter-end backlog: C$158M (up 11% YoY)
- Book-to-bill ratio: 1.27
- 80% of C$158M backlog expected to convert to revenue in 2026

**Segment Sales**
- FTG Aerospace: C$17M (up 12%)
- FTG Circuits: C$31M (up 8%)

**Margin Headwinds**
- Unfavorable CAD/USD exchange rate
- One-time impacts from FX and gold contract realization
- Tariffs also pressured margins; passing costs to customers with some lag

**FTG Circuits**
- Qualified for 2 new defense programs last year; initial orders placed in Q1
- Deliveries expected from Q3 FY26, bigger chunk in FY27
- Supported Artemis mission — supplied switch interface panels for Orion spacecraft

**FTG Aerospace (Calgary)**
- Secured a large order in Q1, contributing to strong book-to-bill
- Flight licensing revenue to Airbus has returned; recurring stream every quarter going forward
- Satcom radios: strong demand; actively insourcing manufacturing to improve margins
- Wireless Quick Access Recorder: certifications done, first delivery complete; quoting new opportunities; pursuing China market
- Weather sensors: momentum slightly behind, but active contracts in US and UK to focus on this year

**Market Tailwinds**
- Airbus targeting 870 deliveries in 2026 (up 10%), ramping to 1,000/year. Big backlog.
- Boeing shipped 600 planes last year, ramping to 800. Backlog at 6,000 planes.
- Bombardier doing well, entering Canadian military defense market.
- US military budget could increase 50% next year.
- Canada increasing defense spending to 3.5% of GDP.

**Strategic**
- Looking for an acquisition or foot in the door in Europe.
