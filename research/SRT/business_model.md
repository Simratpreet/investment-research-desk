# Business Model

## 1. Business Deep Dive

SRT Marine Systems is a UK-based technology company that provides **integrated maritime domain awareness (MDA) systems** — the digital eyes, ears, and brain that enable a country's coast guard, navy, or fishing authority to see, understand, and respond to everything happening in its waters. Think of it as "air traffic control for the ocean" — a sovereign country buys SRT's platform and can finally see in real-time every vessel in its territorial waters, detect smuggling, prevent illegal fishing, coordinate search and rescue, and protect its maritime borders.

### The Two Divisions

**1. Systems Division (~90% of revenue, and the growth engine)**

This is where the magic — and the money — is. SRT builds and delivers **national-scale maritime surveillance and intelligence systems** to sovereign governments. Each "project" is essentially an entire digital infrastructure buildout for a country:

- **Physical infrastructure:** Radar towers, surveillance stations, command centers, power systems — subcontracted to local construction partners in-country.
- **SRT-MDA System Tech:** The proprietary hardware and software stack. This includes SRT's GeoVS software platform (the core intelligence engine), radar systems, cameras, AIS base stations, and increasingly, unmanned surface vessels (USVs) and UAVs integrated into the network.
- **Training & Organizational Transformation:** SRT doesn't just hand over technology — it trains the operator organization to adopt an "Intelligence-Led Operations" (ILO) doctrine. This is a fundamental shift from "go out on patrol and hope to catch something" to "the system detects, analyzes, and tells you exactly where to go and what to do."
- **Data Services:** Satellite surveillance scans, processed AI data tokens, and supplementary data feeds that augment the core system.
- **Ongoing Support:** Long-term maintenance, software updates, system operations support.

Each sovereign customer is what SRT calls a **"Sovereign Partnership"** — a decade-long relationship where the country progressively builds out its MDA capability through a **series of contracts** (not a single one-and-done deal). The first contract might cover one coastline; the second adds another; the third integrates fisheries management; the fourth adds USV fleets; and so on.

**2. Navigation Safety Division (~10% of revenue, profitable and stable)**

The heritage business. SRT manufactures **AIS transponders** — the devices that go on boats and continuously broadcast their identity, position, speed, and heading. Think of them as a boat's "digital license plate" visible to all other boats and to coastal authorities.

- **Products:** Class A (commercial), Class B (leisure), digital aids to navigation (DAS/DATON buoys for ports/waterways)
- **Distribution:** ~5,000 dealers globally
- **Units shipped:** 500,000+ over the company's history
- **ARPU:** ~$400 per unit
- **Gross margin:** ~42-45%
- **Regulatory tailwinds:** New mandates coming — Hamburg Port, Seychelles, Montenegro all now requiring AIS transponders

**NEXUS — The Big New Product:**
SRT's most ambitious transceiver product, combining AIS + VHF radio + mobile device integration into a single unit. Boats can use their iPhones/watches as marine radios connected through NEXUS. Commenced shipping January 2026 after years of development. Expected to have a 10-15 year product lifecycle.
