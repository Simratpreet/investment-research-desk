# SCODATUBES — Q2 FY26

**Capacity Expansion**

• Scoda Tubes has undertaken a significant capacity expansion in its seamless pipe manufacturing operations, growing its annual production capacity from 10,000 metric tons per annum to 17,000 metric tons per annum.

• Building on this, the company has plans for a further expansion that will increase seamless pipe capacity to 20,000 metric tons per annum.

• In addition to seamless pipes, the company is also undertaking a major expansion in its welded tube segment, scaling capacity significantly from 1,000 metric tons per annum to 13,000 metric tons per annum.

• Management has guided for a steady ramp-up in capacity utilization, targeting 60% utilization by FY26 and further improving to 80% by FY27.

**IPO Proceeds Deployment**

• Regarding the deployment of IPO proceeds, the company has so far utilised 27 crores towards capital expenditure and 50 crores for working capital requirements. A substantial amount of 110 crores still remains parked in the bank, indicating significant financial firepower available for future deployment.

**Products & Applications**

• The company manufactures two distinct product categories — seamless pipes, which are primarily used for fluid transportation in oil and gas projects by EPC contractors, and seamless tubes, which are used in the fabrication of industrial equipment such as boilers, heat exchangers, and condensers. The power sector demand, particularly from end users like NTPC and Adani via long-term contracts with BHEL, is largely driven by stainless steel seamless tubes used in boiler manufacturing.

**Demand Drivers**

• Management is expressing strong confidence in demand from the power sector, with key customers such as BHEL, Adani, and TPC expected to drive robust order flows. This demand is anticipated to come from both the thermal and renewable energy segments, reflecting the broad-based nature of the opportunity.

• On the global front, oil and gas expansion is emerging as a major demand driver for the company's exports, presenting a significant opportunity for Scoda Tubes to grow its international revenue base.

• From an industry demand perspective, both Scoda Tubes and Venus Pipes have indicated that total demand in India exceeds 3 lakh metric tons. Notably, the commentary suggests that a large portion of this demand is currently being served by unorganized or smaller players, and there appears to be a structural shift underway from unorganized to organized players, which could present a meaningful market share opportunity for established companies like Scoda Tubes.

**Margins & Financial Guidance**

• On the margin front, the seamless segment currently enjoys healthy EBITDA margins of 16-18%, while the welded segment operates at relatively lower margins of 12-13%. As the company scales up its welded tube capacity, the overall blended margins are expected to settle in the 15-16% range.

• Management has indicated an overall revenue growth guidance of around 20%, though the commentary on this was somewhat vague and lacked specific detail, making it difficult to hold management firmly to this number.
