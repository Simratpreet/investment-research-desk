# SOI — Concall Q3 Fy26

**Historical Context & Inventory Correction**
* The current market challenges in the mobile and automotive sectors are primarily rooted in a significant inventory correction cycle following the pandemic.
* Prior to COVID-19, annual demand for RF SOI wafers typically held at a baseline of approximately 1 million; however, this figure surged to nearly 2.5 million wafers during the pandemic as inventory began to pile up.
* As demand gradually normalizes, Soitec management is executing a strategy of deliberate under-shipping, a process that is expected to continue until the market stabilizes and shipping volumes return to the original baseline of roughly 1 million wafer chips.

**Market Demand & Growth Drivers**
* The major source of current market demand is being driven significantly by the artificial intelligence sector.
* This surge in AI-related demand is stemming from applications across both edge and cloud computing environments.

**Photonics, FT-SOI & Advanced Optics**
* There is currently a very healthy level of demand for both Photonics SOI and FT-SOI, which serve as critical components supporting these advanced AI applications.
* Photonics SOI acts as a foundational substrate that enables the broader optics demand, functioning as a key material for optical transceivers and various other optical modules.
* As the industry gradually transitions toward the adoption of co-packaged optics (CPO), the specific demand per chip for Photonics SOI is expected to increase significantly.
* Furthermore, Soitec is developing Lithium Niobate on Insulator solutions, which will operate alongside Photonics SOI within the CPO ecosystem to specifically enable ultra-high optical transfer speeds exceeding 1.6 terabits per second.

**Mobile Communications & RF Solutions**
* Within the mobile communications segment, overall demand is currently experiencing a significant dampening effect as a direct result of the aforementioned inventory overhang.
* Despite these broader inventory challenges, active and ongoing discussions are continuing with customers regarding the adoption of advanced radio frequency (RF) solutions.

**Millimeter Wave (mmWave) 5G**
* Positioned as a premium tier of 5G technology, Millimeter Wave is currently found predominantly in flagship smartphone models.
* Demand for this advanced technology, along with Soitec's corresponding product offerings, is actively and steadily penetrating the market.
* While the overall market adoption rate for mmWave is currently on the slower side, it remains an ongoing, continuous area of growth and development.

**Automotive Sector**
* Similarly, the automotive side is also currently facing a period of weak demand due to the industry-wide inventory correction, though management maintains a positive outlook, noting that their products are well-positioned to rebound as broader market demand eventually returns.

**Competitive Landscape & R&D**
* Management has expressed strong confidence in their market position, stating that they are currently operating far ahead of their competitors.
* To secure and maintain this competitive edge, the company has aggressively invested in innovation, effectively doubling their research and development resources over the past three to four years.
* Because of this strong positioning and sustained investment, management does not view the current competitive landscape as a major concern at this time.
