# ETON — Concall Q4 Fy25

- Q4 2025 product revenue grew 83%
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 29% in Q4
- Received approval for **Desmoda**, a drug for the rare disease diabetes insipidus
- Desmoda is a clean-label drug with no age limitation
  - Earlier addressable market: ~3,000–4,000 children in the US
  - Now expanded to include ~9,000–10,000 adults as well
  - Earlier estimated market size: ~$30M in peak sales (pediatric market)
  - Market size now ~2x larger — patient count is ~3x higher, but adult dosage is likely lower, offsetting some of the increase
  - Patent for Desmoda extends until 2044
  - Net average selling price: ~$80,000
- Acquired a drug called **Increlex**, used for children with growth deficiency problems
  - At time of acquisition, Increlex was reaching 67 patients; now reaching ~100 patients; target is ~120 patients by year-end
  - Increlex currently covers fewer children in the US compared to Europe due to stricter/different eligibility criteria in the US
  - Plan to conduct a clinical trial in the US to demonstrate to the FDA that the criteria can be expanded to include more children over time
- Portfolio of drugs in **pediatric endocrinology**
  - Currently hold ~12% market share; targeting 20%
  - Peak annual sales potential: ~$50M — significant headroom for growth
- Acquired **Hemangol** — another rare disease drug for children
  - Treats vascular tumors in babies; if untreated, can lead to vision loss and other serious complications
  - ~5,000–10,000 babies in the US affected per year — another niche addressable market
  - Strategy: rationalize distribution channels through existing rare disease network
  - Only ~400 endocrinologists need to be reached to prescribe this drug — no need for a massive salesforce
- Another drug: **Galzin** — currently doing dosage optimizations and other improvements
  - Currently reaching ~300 patients; addressable market is ~1,000 patients — potential to double sales
  - Peak annual sales potential for the overall portfolio: ~$100M

**Financial Goals & Outlook**
- FY 2026: revenue target of $110M; adjusted EBITDA margin of 30%
- FY 2028: adjusted EBITDA margin target of 50%
- By end of FY 2027: exiting at a run rate of $200M
- Growth drivers: continued growth of **Alkindi**, **Increlex**, **Galzin**; successful integration & relaunch of **Hemangol**; strong launch of **Desmoda**; expected launch of **Khindivi** expanded label in 2027
- Confident in closing at least one more product acquisition providing incremental revenue before end of 2027
- Management emphasizes significant operating leverage ahead
