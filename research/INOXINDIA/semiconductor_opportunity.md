# Semiconductor Opportunity

INOX India is perfectly positioned to be a primary "pick-and-shovel" beneficiary of India's massive semiconductor push. With an estimated **INR 1.6 lakh crores** planned for semiconductor investments in the country (with about 75% of that concentrated in Gujarat), INOX is tapping right into the infrastructure needed to make these fabs operational. 

Here is exactly how they are benefiting from this capex cycle:

### **1. The Underlying Need: Massive Volumes of High-Purity Gases**
Manufacturing semiconductors requires a hyper-clean environment and huge quantities of industrial gases. 
* Fabs use nitrogen for chemical wafer deposition and purging, and oxygen for silicon layer deposition and etching. 
* In total, a semiconductor plant can require up to 47 different types of highly specialized gases.
* Because India is a tropical and naturally dusty environment, maintaining absolute cleanliness and preventing contamination in gas lines is a massive engineering challenge. 

### **2. INOX India's Solutions (The Products)**
INOX India isn't making the microchips; they are manufacturing the highly specialized, ultra-clean cryogenic equipment required to store and transport these gases to the fab tools:
* **Storage Tanks & Vaporizers:** They manufacture flat-bottom tanks (FBTs) and massive cryogenic storage tanks (like 250-cubic-meter vessels and 90KL liquid hydrogen tanks).
* **Ultra-High Purity Ammonia Containers:** They recently developed and dispatched India's first ISO containers specifically built for ultra-high purity ammonia, which is critical for semiconductor and solar manufacturing. 
* **Polished Piping Networks:** A single mega-fab can require up to 50 kilometers of cryogenic pipelines.

### **3. Key Customer Wins & Projects**
INOX India has already secured contracts with almost all the major players setting up shop in India (and some abroad):
* **Tata Electronics:** INOX supplied two 250-cubic-meter tanks, vaporizers, and piping to Tata's Assam facility (an order worth roughly INR 8 crores).
* **Micron:** They have supplied equipment to Micron's facilities in both the USA and Dholera, Gujarat.
* **Foxconn:** They supplied equipment to Foxconn's facility in Bangalore.
* **INOX Air Products (Sister Company):** INOX Air Products is building a dedicated industrial gas plant in Dholera specifically to serve the semiconductor hub; INOX India will be supplying the storage and transport equipment for this massive project.

### **4. Financial Impact**
This is no longer just a futuristic narrative for the company; it is already moving the needle on their financials. As of Q2 FY26, management stated that the semiconductor segment already accounts for **8% to 10% of INOX India's total revenue**. 

Because there are virtually no other domestic manufacturers capable of building cryogenic equipment to these ultra-clean specifications, INOX India expects to capture the lion's share of this market as the ecosystem develops over the next 3 to 5 years.
