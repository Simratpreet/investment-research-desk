Here is a detailed breakdown of INOX India’s key products, what they do, their estimated ASPs, and their respective TAMs.

### **1. LNG Fuel Tanks & Mobility Systems**
* **What it does & How it helps:** These are onboard cryogenic storage tanks that replace traditional diesel tanks on heavy-duty commercial vehicles (trucks, buses) and railway locomotives. They allow commercial fleets to transition to LNG, a cleaner fuel that reduces CO2 emissions by 25% and particulate matter by 95%. Using two 450-liter tanks or larger 990-liter tanks, trucks can travel up to 1,000 to 2,000 kilometers on a single fill, eliminating range anxiety for fleet operators.
* **Estimated ASP:** **INR 5 Lakhs per tank.** Management explicitly confirmed that an average LNG fuel tank is sold for around INR 5 lakhs.
* **TAM (India & Global):** In India, the company expects a demand of 30,000 to 40,000 fuel tanks annually within the next 3 to 5 years as heavy-duty LNG adoption scales. At INR 5 lakhs per unit, this implies a near-term domestic TAM of **INR 1,500 to 2,000 Crores per year**. Globally, the overarching cryogenic equipment market (of which LNG storage holds a dominant ~30% share) is valued at roughly USD 23 to 26 Billion currently, providing massive export headroom.

### **2. Stainless Steel Beverage Kegs**
* **What it does & How it helps:** High-grade, FSSC-approved stainless steel containers for storing, transporting, and dispensing beer. They help global brewing giants (like Heineken and AB InBev) overcome severe logistical shortages and replace aging keg inventories.
* **Estimated ASP:** **~INR 5,400 per keg.** We can derive this because INOX generated INR 27 Crores in revenue from selling just over 50,000 kegs in FY25.
* **TAM (India & Global):** There is a global active pool of 120 million kegs, with an annual replacement demand of 4% to 5%. This translates to a global requirement of 4 million to 5 million new kegs every year. At our calculated ASP, the global replacement TAM is approximately **INR 2,160 to 2,700 Crores annually**. 

### **3. Disposable Industrial Cylinders**
* **What it does & How it helps:** Single-use cylinders utilized for transporting industrial gases, helium, and refrigerants (like R32). They help plug structural supply chain deficits, particularly for US clients who face domestic manufacturing capacity constraints.
* **Estimated ASP:** **~INR 920 per cylinder.** Management noted doing roughly INR 46 Crores in revenue by selling 0.5 million (500,000) cylinders to National Refrigeration.
* **TAM (India & Global):** The US market alone experiences a structural shortage where demand exceeds local production by 1.5 million to 2 million units annually. This immediate gap alone represents a highly accessible **INR 138 to 184 Crore** opportunity for INOX, while the broader global industrial packaging market is significantly larger. 

### **4. Semiconductor & Ultra-High Purity Gas Systems**
* **What it does & How it helps:** Highly specialized storage tanks (like 90KL liquid hydrogen tanks or Ultra-High Purity Ammonia ISO containers) built to maintain absolute cleanliness. The semiconductor and solar manufacturing sectors require entirely dust-free, contaminant-free gases for delicate processes like chemical wafer deposition and silicon layering.
* **Estimated ASP:** **INR 4 Crores to 12 Crores per project.** ASP varies heavily based on volume. However, INOX supplied two 250-cubic-meter tanks to a Tata semiconductor project in Assam for roughly INR 8 Crores (so ~INR 4 Crores per tank). Broader supply contracts with associated piping and vaporizers (like for Dholera) run about INR 10 to 12 Crores.
* **TAM (India & Global):** In India, the government and private sector are planning a massive **INR 1.6 Lakh Crore** investment outlay for semiconductor infrastructure. While INOX only captures the cryogenic gas handling portion of this, it is a highly lucrative, high-margin niche. Globally, electronics/semiconductors represent the fastest-growing end-user segment for cryogenic equipment.

### **5. Small-Scale LNG Terminals**
* **What it does & How it helps:** Complete, localized LNG storage and regasification terminals built for island nations (e.g., Bahamas, Caribbean) or off-grid industrial areas. It allows isolated regions to pivot away from expensive and dirty diesel power generation to cleaner LNG.
* **Estimated ASP:** **~INR 200 Crores.** Management noted that upcoming bids for island terminals in places like Indonesia and the Philippines are valued at around INR 200 Crores each.
* **TAM (India & Global):** This is a multi-billion dollar global market. In India, smaller-scale satellite stations are booming; there are roughly 88 satellite stations in the country, and INOX has supplied 64 of them (a 73% market share). 

### **6. Scientific & Nuclear Fusion Infrastructure**
* **What it does & How it helps:** Highly complex thermal shields (like the Cryostat Thermal Shield or Vacuum Vessel Thermal Shield) used to contain the extreme temperatures of experimental nuclear fusion reactors (like the ITER project in France).
* **Estimated ASP:** **~INR 145 Crores.** INOX secured a major repair/refurbishment order for the Cryostat Thermal Shield at ITER valued at approximately INR 145 Crores.
* **TAM (India & Global):** Very niche but highly recurring for approved vendors. INOX estimates its addressable market just for global fusion projects to be **INR 500 to 600 Crores** over the next 5 years (roughly INR 100 to 150 Crores annually).

### **7. Next-Generation Energy Storage (CO2 & Liquid Air)**
* **What it does & How it helps:** Storage systems that use liquid air or capture CO2 to create long-duration energy storage "batteries". They serve as a highly scalable, environmentally friendly alternative to traditional lithium-ion batteries for power grids.
* **Estimated ASP:** Still in the pilot phase. An initial order of 26 units of 50-cubic-meter tanks was described as "not a very big order in value-wise", likely translating to a few crores in total.
* **TAM (India & Global):** Currently unquantifiable as the technology is in its infancy globally, but it targets the trillion-dollar grid-scale renewable energy storage market.
